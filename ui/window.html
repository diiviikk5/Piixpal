The train window with its wall cut out becomes a window you can drop next to your copy. The rest of the page stays ordinary, so the window is the one thing that moves.

<!-- once, anywhere on the page: every component -->
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/piixpal@0.6/piixpal.min.js"></script>
<!-- just window (the shared engine loads itself, once) -->
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/piixpal@0.6/dist/c/scene.min.js"></script>
npm install piixpal
