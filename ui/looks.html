Pixel UI/Get started

Looks

The glow button, glow card, input and dialog all take a look. It swaps the whole style, from the shader to the type, so one set of components can live on very different pages.

Glow

The default. A dark pixel face, a band of light running round the border and a spotlight under the pointer. At home on dark product pages.

Riso

A two-ink risograph print: a solid ink face, a halftone shadow on two edges and a second ink laid down slightly off-register. It drifts when you hover. Give it any two inks and a paper as its palette.
