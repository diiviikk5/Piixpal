Moonroof

What you see when you finally look up at a red light.

There is a lamp at the edge of town that nobody remembers putting up. It stands in a field, wired to nothing anyone can find, and every night at a quarter past eleven it comes on.

The drivers who pass it say the sky bends round it. They say it the way you say something you know sounds silly, and then they say it again.

We went to look. We stood in the field until the light came on, and then we stopped talking for a while.
