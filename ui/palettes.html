Your own colours

Pass two to six colours, dark to light, separated by commas. The darkest is the background, the lightest the highlights.

<piix-shader kind="warp" palette="#0b0a10,#3a2a6b,#ff6b4a,#ffe8a8" height="240px"></piix-shader>

Dither

Between two palette colours the pixels are ordered-dithered with an 8×8 Bayer matrix, which is what gives everything its texture. Turn it down with dither, from 1 (full) to 0 (flat bands).

<div class="ex-row">
  <piix-shader kind="mesh" dither="1" height="160px"></piix-shader>
  <piix-shader kind="mesh" dither=".5" height="160px"></piix-shader>
  <piix-shader kind="mesh" dither="0" height="160px"></piix-shader>
</div>
