Holo card

A holographic trading card that tilts under your hand.

<piix-holo-card>DOM + canvasNew

Nova

Legendary · #042

<piix-holo-card style="width:250px">
  <img src="showcase/nova.jpg" alt="">
  <h3>Nova</h3>
  <p>Legendary · #042</p>
</piix-holo-card>
Hue

Like a rare foil card: it tilts toward the pointer in 3D while a rainbow foil slides across it at the angle of the light, sparkles glint in the foil, a glare follows you and faint scanlines remind you it's projected. On a phone, tap to wobble it.
